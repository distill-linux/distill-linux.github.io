---
title: "special thanks"
description: "acknowledgments and appreciation to the projects and communities that make distill possible"
---

<p>distill linux stands on the shoulders of remarkable independent software projects, maintainers, and hackers. we extend our deepest gratitude to the following communities and creators:</p>

<details open>
<summary>void linux & contributors</summary>
<p>for engineering <a href="https://voidlinux.org/">void linux</a>, the upstream base of distill. the brilliance of the <strong>xbps</strong> package manager, clean source templates, and elegant <strong>runit</strong> integration are the backbone of our system.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>d&eacute;rive (derivelinux.org)</summary>
<p>for providing direct inspiration for our website design and philosophy. <a href="https://derivelinux.org/">d&eacute;rive</a> demonstrates the raw power and beauty of independent, unbloated operating system design.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>x11libre team</summary>
<p>for modernizing and preserving the x11 display server with <a href="https://github.com/X11Libre/xserver">X11Libre</a>, bringing <code>seatd</code> support and TearFree rendering without systemd hard dependencies.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>toybox (rob landley & contributors)</summary>
<p>for developing <a href="https://landley.net/toybox/">toybox</a> under the 0-bsd license, giving us a clean, unified, fast, and bloat-free implementation of posix core utilities.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>mksh / mirbsd (thorsten glaser & team)</summary>
<p>for the <a href="https://www.mirbsd.org/mksh.htm">mirbsd korn shell (mksh)</a>, an exceptionally reliable, standards-conforming, and lean default shell.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>musl libc (rich felker & community)</summary>
<p>for creating <a href="https://musl.libc.org/">musl libc</a>, the premier lightweight and standards-compliant c library for modern unix systems.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>fsfla & linux-libre team</summary>
<p>for maintaining <a href="https://www.fsfla.org/ikiwiki/selibre/linux-libre/">linux-libre</a>, tirelessly scrubbing proprietary binary blobs to ensure computing remains 100% free software.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>runit (gerrit pape)</summary>
<p>for creating <a href="http://smarden.org/runit/">runit</a>, a fast, simple, and rock-solid init and process supervision scheme.</p>
</details>

<details open>
<summary>cat-v.org & werc</summary>
<p>for pioneering the timeless, minimal, and functional web aesthetic that inspires communities across the libre software world.</p>
</details>
